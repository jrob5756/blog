### **1. [AWS Emulator](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/aws-gives-developers-a-free-local-stand-in-for-its-cloud-built-from-scratch-by-ai-agents/)**

**Source**: Techmeme  
**Date**: November 11, 2026

**Detailed Summary**: AWS released a free local emulator for 200+ AWS services. AWS says autonomous agents wrote it under human-designed specifications, fidelity tests, and merge gates. It speeds local and CI testing but is not for production, IAM, security, performance, or regional validation.

### **2. [A2UI 1.0](https://developers.googleblog.com/a2ui-10-build-delightful-fast-reliable-agentic-experiences/)**

**Source**: DevURLs  
**Date**: November 11, 2026

**Detailed Summary**: Google's open A2UI protocol lets agents produce validated native forms, cards, charts, and approvals from approved component catalogs. It adds function calls, accessibility, SDKs, and MCP Apps interoperability.

### **3. [ExecuTorch WebGPU](https://pytorch.org/blog/executorch-webgpu-pytorch-models-on-the-gpu-in-the-browser/)**

**Source**: Hacker News (PyTorch Blog)  
**Date**: November 11, 2026

**Detailed Summary**: PyTorch added a WebGPU backend for local browser inference from `.pte` artifacts. It reports faster LLM execution than its WebAssembly CPU path, but remains early-stage.
